About LifeLink

LifeLink is a web-based blood donation and donor connection system designed to connect blood donors with people who need blood.

The platform helps users find donors according to blood group and location. It aims to make finding blood donors easier, faster, and more accessible.

Our Services

  • Blood Donor Search
  • Donor Registration
  • Blood Group-Based Search
  • Location-Based Donor Search
  • Donor Contact Information
  • User Login
  • Administration and Donor Management

How It Works

  1. Donors register on the LifeLink website.
  2. Donor information is stored in the system.
  3. People who need blood can search for donors without creating an account.
  4. Users select a blood group and enter their location to find suitable donors.
  5. Users can contact available donors using the contact information provided.
